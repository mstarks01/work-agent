How questions work

The page lists the recommended questions, the most useful first. Each answer is saved as you give it, and the page says when a save fails or an answer changed elsewhere. Answers can add questions as well as remove them, and the page says when they do. Blank questions stay in the list. Skip for now sets a question aside, where you can answer it later. “None,” “I don’t know,” and a blank answer have different meanings. Only Start the analysis runs the analysis. The report may offer one optional follow-up.

Analysis frameworks

Questions are dynamically generated. Your answers may generate more or answer several at once.

A “no” removes from the analysis the checks that need that part of the application. Where you are not sure, leave the question blank or choose “I don't know”.

Skipped and unanswered questions stay open.
