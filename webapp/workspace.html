How questions work

Questions arrive in rounds. The progress bar counts answers in this round; a multi-part question has several answers. The remaining total is an estimate because answers can add or remove questions. Save keeps your answers; blank questions may return. Skip sets unanswered questions aside, where you can answer them later. “None,” “I don’t know,” and a blank answer have different meanings. Only Start the analysis runs the analysis. The report may offer one optional follow-up.
