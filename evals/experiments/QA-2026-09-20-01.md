## Answers

**What is hurting report quality?** On the evidence available offline, the lane
agents. Two thirds of every missed STRIDE reference is a place the lane never
cited (`place`, `unled`), and most of those have a draft sitting one element
over. That is a generation problem, not a merge, critic or extraction one.

**Which phase is responsible, how confidently, and what alternatives remain?**
Analysis, with moderate confidence, and preparation is the live alternative: a
`place` row means a candidate rule led the lane there and nothing was drafted,
which is consistent both with a lane that read the lead badly and with a lead
that arrived badly. The two are separable offline by reading the assembled lane
material per row, and that was not done here.

Two phases were ruled out. **Criticism**: the critic killed 5 drafts of 244 and
lost 1 must-find for good. **The deterministic half of extraction**: a perfect
reading of all 13 signed cases survives the path 122 rows of 122, losing none.
What a model reads is untested by that; what the code does with it is not the
bottleneck.

**What is the proposed fix?** None yet, deliberately. The largest class is not
narrow enough to price, and this audit's authorisation is diagnosis.

**What was changed, and what was measured?** Nothing was changed. Three
Baselines were re-scored, the oracle and the bottleneck instruments were run,
and the loss blocks were read.

**What did not work?** Four refuted rows. `-E2` and `-E3` rule out two phases:
the critic is not destroying the missing findings and the deterministic path is
not dropping them. `-E4` and `-E5` correct this audit's own first reading of
priority 1 — the comparability crossing is flagged by the tooling, and the drop
is a frozen artifact rather than scorer drift.

**What remains untested or blocked?** The split between analysis and
preparation for the `place` class. Reviewed precision, which needs votes.
Report prose, which no instrument reads. Anything about ASVS, which has no
merged Baseline.

**What was spent, and what remains?** $0 spent. The budget was $0.

**What should the next audit do first?** Take the 59 `place` rows and read, per
row, what the lane was given and what it drafted instead. That is offline and
free, and it is what splits priority 2 into two answerable questions. Read the
ledger first: `-E4` and `-E5` are why priority 1 is not the place to start.

Every one reads lower. The first reading of that was wrong twice, and the

### The first reading of this was wrong, in two ways

**The crossing is not silent.** `run.py score` prints, in full:

```
NOT COMPARABLE TO THIS ARTIFACT'S OWN FIGURES: it ran on corpus b09805c6726d
and these numbers come from d5f345c2eedb.
```

The first pass filtered the command's output for the coverage lines and did not
see it. An instrument is read whole or it is not read. That is
`QA-2026-09-20-01-E4`, refuted.

**The drop is not scorer drift.** Diffing which `(case, reference_index)` pairs
matched on each side says which way the movement went:

| Baseline | corpus digest | lost | gained |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `2198870` | `b09805c6726d` | 16 | 0 |
| `d3f1898` | `b09805c6726d` | 14 | 0 |
| `d4edd64` | `09772afde754` | 14 | 5 |

A reference correction moves matches both ways. A one-way loss is the signature
of something that can never match: a sealed artifact citing element and flow
identifiers the corpus has since moved. The two Baselines on one digest lose and
gain nothing; the third, on its own digest, moves both ways. That is
`QA-2026-09-20-01-E5`, refuted.

So the conclusion is narrower and more useful than the first one. **A re-score
of a sealed Baseline is a record, not a measurement of today's quality**, and
nothing in the code needs changing. What the repository needs is a Baseline cut
on the current corpus digest `d5f345c2eedb`, at which point none of this
arithmetic is necessary.

The whole correction cost nothing and came from reading the instrument again.
It is the clearest argument for the ledger in this report: the next audit that
reaches this signature is handed both refutations before it starts.
