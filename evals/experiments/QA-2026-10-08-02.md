## E1: the critic uses the field (supported)

With the new prompt, 60 of 66 STRIDE question-kind entries name facets (91%),
and 21 of 27 ASVS entries (78%). Most name one or two facets. Nobody has read
yet whether the named facets are the right ones.

## E2: the effect on verdicts (inconclusive)

Only STRIDE cases 01 to 04 have both sides. On those 60 drafts, the two prompts
agree on 59 (0.983). The one move is a needs-info draft that the new prompt
confirmed. That is inside the run-to-run spread measured earlier (about 3%,
QA-2026-09-24-02-E12), but four cases are too few to settle the question.

## E3: the change moves verdicts (refuted; supersedes E2)

After a credit top-up, the missing calls ran one at a time. The run spent
$1.61 in all. The old-prompt call for case 12 did not run, because the
approved $1.60 was reached.

| | Paired drafts | Agree | Moves, old to new |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| STRIDE, 12 cases | 179 | 170 (0.950) | 8 needs-info to confirmed, 1 confirmed to needs-info |
| ASVS case 01 | 203 | 139 (0.685) | 64 confirmed to rejected |

**The ASVS moves are a regression.** All 64 are lane drafts that say a
requirement does not apply because the model shows no such component (for
example 1.2.5: no OS-command interpreter). ADR 0027 allows an exclusion on
absence, and the old prompt confirmed them. The new prompt rejects every one
for `evidence`, with reasons like "No LDAP subject is modeled".

**The moves come from the prompt, not from chance.** On these drafts the ASVS
critic's verdicts repeat exactly for a given prompt. The new side equals the
2026-10-05 replay with prompts at `a4df83cd` (203 of 203), and the old side
equals the replay at `25c46d24` (203 of 203). The one commit between those two
is #1480, which edited the critic's paragraph on the forms of an open fact.
#1553 extended the same paragraph.

The STRIDE disagreement (5%) is a little above the earlier measured spread
(about 3%), and it moves drafts toward confirmed. That spread was measured
under an older prompt.

## Clarifying run: E4 to E6 (supersede the reading of E3)

The maintainer approved about $2.05, to stop early once the results were
definitive. The run stopped at $0.88.

**The ASVS moves in E3 were a fix, not a regression.** In ASVS, `confirmed`
says a requirement applies, and a rejection for `evidence` says it does not.
An exclusion draft is written so that the critic rejects it. The prompt before
#1553 confirmed exclusions on absence: 64 of 75 on the October drafts and 93 of
93 on the September drafts of case 01. The archived reports of 2026-09-10
rejected 89 of 93 and confirmed none, so the defect began with #1480.

**E4, supported: an explicit rule fixes it.** `frameworks/asvs/critic.md` now
says an excluded draft is never confirmed. With it, both prompts reject every
exclusion on both draft sets (75 and 93), and the other drafts keep their
verdicts.

**E5, refuted: the facet prompt does not move STRIDE beyond normal variation.**
The prompt before #1553 disagreed with its own first pass on 10 of 133 verdicts
(7.5%), seven of them toward confirmed. The facet prompt's shift was 5%.

**E6, refuted: the empty ASVS answers are not systematic.** With the raw answer
kept (#1558), the same configuration ruled on every draft, twice. The two
earlier empty answers stay unexplained.
