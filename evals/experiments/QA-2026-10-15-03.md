## Answers

**What is hurting report quality?** The same three conventions as
`QA-2026-10-15-02` named. The prompt wording of this audit did not carry them.

**Which phase is responsible?** Extraction for the mixed IDs of case 14. The
audit itself for the host option: its text said the host load is not a flow,
and the corpus holds that flow.

**What was changed, and what was measured?** E1: three sentences in
`extract.md` on `rule-4-and-6-rulings-prompt` (commit `8a212450`), sweeps at
`d2c67438` with case 13 and 14 notes, five sweeps on luna. E2 and E3: the
corrected sentences on `rule-4-host-and-third-party`, three and five sweeps.
The gate repair shipped as #1749 (ADR 0089). No prompt branch is merged. See
the evidence table.

**What did not work?** E1, refuted on element loss and must-find exposure,
null on the tag figure. E1's row gives the figures of four sweeps, because the
replay could read sweep 1 only after #1741. Row E4 gives the five-sweep
figures that the evidence table shows. E2 is refuted, and E3 is null.

**What remains untested or blocked?** Case 14's
`return-with-authorization-code` flow, lost 5 of 5 on every arm. An arm that
isolates one word cut in `extract.md`, which needs a higher token cap. The
live effect of the gate repair on a job.

**What was spent, and what remains?** $0.789 of $0.90 over three grants: E1
$0.240, E2 $0.309 (a $0.008 pre-flight and three sweeps at OpenAI list price),
E3 $0.240. Attempts 3 of 9.

**What needs fresh model output, and which decision does it change?** Nothing
for these rulings. Three arms say a prompt sentence moves the loss and does not
remove it; a further arm on the same sentences answers no open decision.

**What should the next audit do first?** Leave rules 4 and 6 as #1735 and
#1734 merged them. Treat case 14's `return-with-authorization-code` flow, lost
5 of 5 on every arm, as its own question. Price a word cut in `extract.md`
before relying on it: the tag moved +2.4 sd between two arms that differ by a
reword, two sentences and two cuts, and nothing separates the three.
