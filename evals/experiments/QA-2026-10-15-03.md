null on the tag figure. The row's figures came from four sweeps; the five-sweep
figures above came after the replay fix of #1741 and point the same way.

partner-app sentence that binds the ID, in one arm. A gate repair that
re-keys an endpoint whose slug exists under one type, as a ticket.

**What was spent, and what remains?** $0.240 of $0.25. Attempts 1 of 3.

of five sweeps, about $0.25, for the two corrected sentences. It decides
whether rulings 2 and 3 ship by prompt or wait for the gate repair.
