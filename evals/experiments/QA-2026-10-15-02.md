## Answers

**What is hurting report quality?**

Extraction loses about 6.5% of the blessed elements a sweep, and the losses
sit on flows that carry must-find claims. Before this audit, 54 of 191
must-find claims touched an element lost in at least one of five sweeps.
Three causes own most of them: rule 4 applied both ways (22 claims), names
that depart from the source (about 20 claims, 10 on case 15), and other
omitted flows (about 12). On matched elements the wrong facts are mostly
`assets`, which three STRIDE rules read as a lead.

**Which phase is responsible, how confidently, and what alternatives remain?**

Extraction, with high confidence for the element losses: the replay reads
the node's own emissions, and the corpus is the other reader. The corpus
owns part of each cause: case 16 applied rule 4 the other way, case 15 kept
an aggregate name, and the seven stores of E4 carry a tag the narrowed rule
does not give. No alternative phase remains for a lost element.

**What is the proposed fix?**

Four levers, all shipped: alias rulings for case 15 (#1726), case 16's
reference re-routed to the app convention (#1727), rule 4 reworded so an app
with behaviour of its own is a Process and the person's flow ends at it
(#1735), and rule 6 narrowed so `business-critical-data` is the operating
records no other tag names (#1734).

**What was changed, and what was measured?**

E1 and E2 changed the corpus and were measured on archived output. E3 and E4
changed `extract.md` and were measured with fresh model output, five sweeps a
side on luna over OpenRouter: E3 moved elements lost by −1.3 sd and must-find
exposure by −1.5 sd; E4 moved wrong `assets` rows by −2.5 sd. The arms are
archived as `evals/emissions/20261015T-rule4-app-process` and
`evals/emissions/20261015T-rule6-bcd` (#1737).

**What did not work?**

Nothing was refuted. E4's narrowing trades over-tagging for under-tagging:
the tag is dropped where the corpus has it on seven elements in every sweep.
The net figure improved, and the convention needs a ruling.

**What remains untested or blocked?**

The live downstream effect of every lever. The case 13 `open-console` flow
and the case 14 partner-app flows stay lost and need rulings. Case 16 is
unread and holds an unsigned placement row. The rule 6 arm lost three case
09 fax flows in 3 of 5 sweeps, which the before arm never lost; the element
loss figure sits inside the spread, and the cause is unknown.

**What was spent, and what remains?**

$0.477 of $0.75: E3 $0.239 and E4 $0.238, each with a $0.003 one-case
pre-flight. $0.273 remains. Attempts: 3 of 3 used.

**What needs fresh model output, and which decision does it change?**

An end-to-end sweep would show whether the recovered flows become findings;
it decides nothing this audit can act on, so it is not asked for. A terra
sweep in analysis mode would refresh the must-find figure; the figure is 68%
offline today and no decision waits on it.

**What should the next audit do first?**

Ask the maintainer for three rulings: which stores carry
`business-critical-data` (case 03 claims warehouse, case 06 player database,
case 10 CDN bucket), whether a person's flow to a served console ends at the
console or at the host (case 13), and whether a partner app that calls an API
is an External Entity or a Process (case 14). Then price rule 4's host
sentence against those rulings.
