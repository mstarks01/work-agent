## Answers

**What is hurting report quality?** In extraction, a few facts change which
STRIDE rule leads a lane. Two crossings into a `privilege` or `tenant` zone are
undecided because the extraction places the flow's source in no zone, and one
vendor assurance phrase becomes a stated control. In the measurement, the loss
table of every archived sweep blamed the lanes for a field they could not
state. #2200 fixed the measurement.

**Which phase is responsible, how confidently, and what alternatives
remain?** Extraction for the three leads, with high confidence on the leads and
no measurement of the matches. A lane may find the finding without the lead.

**What is the proposed fix?** For the crossings: a ruling on whether
`_privilege_zone_crossing` fires on an undecided crossing whose destination
zone is `privilege` or `tenant`. Its docstring and ADR 0039 rule 4 say it must
not, because the source may sit in the same zone. For case 12: a rule in
`prompts/extract.md` that an assurance phrase that names no mechanism sets no
attribute, which `corrections.md` row 4 states for the case.

**What was changed, and what was measured?** #2200: the `unstated` cause. E1
to E4 measured the archive.

**What did not work?** E1 refuted the old reading of 54 of 191. E3 refuted the
estimate of about 9 open near misses: the #1945 sitting ruled 6 of them as
different findings.

**What remains untested or blocked?** Whether a lane matches the three
references without the lead. Whether a stronger lane model recovers the
analysis-phase `verb` misses (no row; it needs provider credit).

**What was spent, and what remains?** $0 of $0.

**What needs fresh model output, and which decision does it change?** Nothing
in this audit. A paid extraction pair on an `extract.md` edit would decide
whether to ship it.

**What should the next audit do first?** Read the wrong facts on found
elements by the must-finds whose leads they move, starting with
`flow.operations` and `flow.authentication`, which the STRIDE rules read.
