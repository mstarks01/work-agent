# QA-2026-10-13-04: one question for each group, priced at matched choices

ADR 0081 asks each group of field questions once for every element a box
lists. This audit prices it offline (`ANALYSIS_OFFLINE=1`, $0) before the page
was built.

## The walk (E1)

`run.py early-policies` gains the policy `group-question`: in each list after
a save, a run of rows that share a group is one charge, at the most parts any
of its rows has, and it answers every row of the run. A capability question is
charged on its own. The other policies are charged as before, and reproduce
`QA-2026-10-13-02/early-policies-grouped-kinds.json` in every cell.
`early-policies-group-question.json`, every answer "I don't know":

| ASVS with STRIDE, settled | 162 → 240 | 184 → 768 | 560 → 1,047 | 723 → 1,268 | 1,120 → 1,595 |

The whole list costs 3,125 choices where it cost 8,739 (STRIDE alone) and
6,947 where it cost 12,666 (both selected). No cell falls.

The walk uses the list the shipped limit builds, and counts a shared answer as
right for every element. It cannot see an owner who answers for an element the
answer does not fit.
