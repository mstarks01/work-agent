## Answers

**What is hurting report quality?**

On today's prompt, extraction finds or renames about 94% of the blessed
elements on the development cases (1,006 of 1,074 element-runs over five
sweeps). The systematic losses are few and named. Two flows are lost in every
run because the model writes the actor's interaction to a UI process it adds
(a dashboard, a console, a phone app) instead of to the component the corpus
names. On case 16 that one flow carried three must-finds in the one end-to-end
run. The other recurring losses are measurement: zones the signed facts call
inferred, and two plurals the sources use. Wrong facts are dominated by
`assets`, which a definitions edit did not move.

**Which phase is responsible, how confidently, and what alternatives remain?**

Finding 2 is extraction, at `extract`, with high confidence: the run carries
the interaction and places it elsewhere. Findings 1, 3 and 4 are the
instrument and the corpus, not a phase. No alternative explanation remains for
1, 3 and 4. For 2, the alternative is that the corpus reading is the one to
change, and that is a ruling.

**What is the proposed fix?**

1. Filter holdout cases out of `replay` through `diagnosable`, with a test.
2. Decide the reading for an application an actor loads: a rule in
   `extract.md`, or a corpus ruling. Batch any prompt edit.
3. Record the inferred placements of cases 01, 04 and 09 as assumptions, or
   add the lint.
4. Pair `respelled` in the alignment, or record two aliases.

**What was changed, and what was measured?** In audit mode, nothing was
changed. Two archives were replayed under today's code, one end-to-end pair
was read through `extraction-losses`, and the suite and the corpus lint ran.
The section "Implement mode" below lists the changes that shipped after.

**What did not work?** No experiment in this audit was refuted. The prior
refuted rows consulted are listed below.

**What remains untested or blocked?** The live effect of any `extract.md`
rule for finding 2. The `flow.operations` and `flow.protocol` wrong facts are
partly charged by the `sole` pairing by design and were not separated here.

**What was spent, and what remains?** $0.2439 of $0.30, all on E10's five
sweeps a side. $0.0561 remains. Attempts: 5 of 6 used.

**What needs fresh model output, and which decision does it change?**
Nothing now. A rule for finding 2 would need five runs a side once it is
written and batched; its ceiling alone sits inside the band.

**What should the next audit do first?**

  It is the only archive on today's prompt, and `evals/runs/` is untracked,
