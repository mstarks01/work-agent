## The question

#744 asks for five analysis runs of each holdout case on one execution
identity, to read the spread. The plan used Claude on the `anthropic` row:
base `claude-haiku-4-5`, strong `claude-opus-5-5` at `high`.

Cases 14 and 15 were exhausted holdouts before these runs. The ledger read
each of them three times (`QA-2026-10-11-02-E1`, `QA-2026-10-12-01-E1`,
`QA-2026-10-14-02-E1`). So E4 measures two development cases, and no row reads
a holdout. The plan did not check `experiments --holdouts` before the runs.
