## Answers

**What does the second call reach?** Only a must-find that sits on a unit the
lane left unanswered. On Baseline 6bff717, re-scored today, `place` and
`unled` lose 25 must-finds. At the scorer's grain, 8 of them sit on an
unanswered unit. 10 share only one element with an unanswered unit, and 7 have
no unanswered unit near them. So the ceiling is 8, not the 33 that ADR 0030
priced on the first Baseline.

**Did the second call beat a plain repeat?** No, by the rule declared before
the run. It won on one row of four:

| 01 ref 14: flood the settlement webhook | yes | **3 of 3** | 1 of 3 (E5 of `QA-2026-09-24-02`) |

**What does that mean?** The second call works where the lane's miss is
variance and the unit sits on the reference's place. It does not change what
the lane believes about a place. On 07 ref 1 the lane writes the opposite
direction whether it is asked once or twice. It cannot reach a place that no
rule offers, as on 07 ref 18.

**What does it cost?** A second call cost about the same as a repeat: $0.0205
against $0.0210 a call. It added 14 drafts over 12 calls, and 10 of them came
from one lane with five open units (07 spoofing). The critic and the votes
would decide whether those drafts are real. This audit did not measure that.

## The prototype wording

The second call was the captured lane request, rebuilt by
`lane_replay.compose`, with one more user part. The script was never merged.
The part said:

> Your first pass filed the drafts listed under "First drafts". Do not file
> any of them again, and do not restate one of them at another place. The
> deterministic analysis offered your lane the units listed under "Unanswered
> units". No first draft cites every element of any of them. Examine each
> unit. Where a threat in your lane follows at that unit, file a new draft
> that cites the unit's elements. Where no threat follows, file nothing for
> it. Return only the new drafts. An empty list is a valid answer.

Each candidate line named the rule, the elements and the rule's question. Each
crossing line named the flow, both endpoints and both zones. A crossing on a
flow that a listed candidate already named was left out.
