# QA-2026-10-10-01: does the bounded source review pass recover stated facts?

For [#1003](https://github.com/mstarks01/work-agent/issues/1003): arms C and D
add one source-driven review pass (`reread`, then `apply`) to the graph-first
route (A) and to the facts-first route (B). The 2026-09-17 pilot ran C and D
on five cases under a scorer with five defects, and its artifacts are gone. The
corrected arm scorer had figures for A, B and E only. This audit buys C and D
one repeat over the 13 tuning cases on `openrouter/openai/gpt-5.6-luna-pro`,
and scores all five arms with `run.py score-arms` and `run.py compare-arms`.

The sweep is `evals/emissions/20261010T-arms-CD-luna-pro/`. Case 01 of arm C
is `preflight-C.json`; the other 12 cases are `arm-C.json`. `review_outcomes.py`
in this directory reads what the review pass did off the archived stages.

## E1: the review pass, inconclusive

13 cases, one repeat per arm. A, B and E are the archived 2026-09-17 sweep on
the same model; C and D ran on 2026-10-10 at `6bca662e`. Recall is the
macro-average per-case recall of stated required facts (122 rows).

| arm | recall | aligned | wrong | unruled | found of 122 |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| A | 0.374 | 0.506 | 0.281 | 0.564 | 42 |
| B | 0.290 | 0.314 | 0.339 | 0.508 | 31 |
| C | 0.387 | 0.530 | 0.323 | 0.598 | 42 |
| D | 0.319 | 0.390 | 0.338 | 0.638 | 35 |
| E | 0.350 | 0.404 | 0.288 | 0.496 | 37 |

Cluster bootstrap over cases, 10,000 draws, 0.95 corrected for four planned
comparisons:

| comparison | difference | interval | rows left ahead / right ahead |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| B − A | −0.084 | −0.186 to −0.010 | 1 / 12 |
| C − A | +0.013 | −0.067 to +0.125 | 7 / 7 |
| D − B | +0.029 | −0.019 to +0.085 | 6 / 2 |
| D − C | −0.068 | −0.187 to +0.031 | 7 / 14 |

Gate 1 asks for +5 points with an interval above zero. C − A and D − B both
include zero, so the review pass is **inconclusive** on both routes. D − C
includes zero and leans to C. B − A excludes zero at the corrected level: the
one-call facts-first head loses to the graph-first head on this corpus.

**The cross-tree comparisons are confounded.** The prompt digests of `extract`,
`assert`, `facts` and `repair` all moved between 2026-09-17 and 2026-10-10, so
C − A and D − B compare the review pass and a prompt revision together. Case 12
shows it: C scores 0.429 where A scored 0.000, and the review pass applied no
operation on that case. Only D − C is a same-tree pair. A clean reading of
C − A needs arm A run again on the same tree, about $0.55.

## What the review pass did

| arm | batches | rolled back | applied | unresolved | refused |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| C | 13 | 2 | 13 | 6 | 19 |
| D | 13 | 7 | 34 | 0 | 62 |

One C case (11) proposed nothing. Refusal codes over both arms:
`batch-rolled-back` 36 (the rest of a discarded batch), `dangling-endpoint` 17,
`dangling-subject` 10, `operation-refused` 9, `stale-precondition` 6,
`wrong-subject-type` 3. On the facts-first route more than half of the batches
name an endpoint or a subject the model does not hold, and the applicator
discards the batch whole, as designed.

## Cost and latency, gate 5

The pass adds one call per case. Over 13 cases `reread` charged $0.178 on the
graph-first route (+33% on $0.544) and $0.225 on the facts-first route (+72%
on $0.311). Mean `reread` latency was 36 s per case on C and 50 s on D. Both
arms stay inside 2× A on cost and on serial latency. Every case produced a
valid catalog on both arms (gate 4).

## Reading

The review pass did not show the gain gate 1 asks for, on either route, at one
repeat over 13 cases. The verdict on #1003 is the maintainer's: the figures
support "retain A" and do not support advancing B or D.
