# QA-2026-10-13-01: the open needs-sweep issues on a $3.15 budget

The maintainer approved $3.15 of OpenRouter credit for the open `needs-sweep`
issues. This audit spent $2.70 on one paid experiment (E1, for #661) and ran two
offline readings (E2 for #1283, E3 for #1476). Every paid call ran at
`3d83a93f` on `openrouter/openai/gpt-5.6-terra`, pinned to `openai/flex`.

## E1: the assertion catalog as ASVS lane evidence (#661 Arm 1): refuted

`lane-replay --framework asvs --prepare-today none|signed` on case 01. All 17
lanes ran twice on each side. The four lanes that moved then ran three more
times on each side. The rule, stated before the runs: a unit counts as changed
only when its direction differs between the arms in every pass, and never
differs between two passes of one arm.

No must-find unit of case 01 changes direction in any pass. After two passes,
12 units met the rule. After five passes, none does.

| Unit | Record | No catalog, 5 passes | Signed facts, 5 passes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| V8.2.1 | none | question/code 4, gap 1 | gap 5 |
| V8.3.1 | none | gap 4, question/code 1 | gap 5 |
| V4.1.1 | expected, needs-code | filed 2 | filed 4 |
| V4.4.1 to V4.4.4 | none | not filed 3, question 2 | question 3, excluded 1, not filed 1 |

V8.2.1 is the one lead (one-sided Fisher p = 0.024). Every signed gap on it
cites the signed `authorization-grant` row: anything that can reach the order
service may submit an order. The system model has no field for this fact.
Case 01 has no V8.2.1 record, so no scorer counts the change.

In the two full passes, the signed arm filed 252 and 251 units against 241 and
245, and 68 and 71 exclusions against 78 and 79.

Limits: lanes only, before the critic and the fan-in. The signed facts are the
most a pass can write, so this is a ceiling and not what luna's rows give.

Reported charges: $2.6977 for 91 lane calls, the pre-flight included.

## E2: #1283's design on case 03 cannot detect a restore: refuted

Offline. Reference 3 of case 03 (must-find, alter) is matched in 6 of 19
archived analysis runs on terra, 2 of 5 in the 2026-09-12 band, and 0 of 2 on
luna. The full-source arm is the ceiling of the test, so five runs an arm
cannot separate a restore from the spread. One withheld run started and was
stopped before it wrote output.

## E3: the pre-#1470 ASVS spread scores today: supported

Offline. `run.py migrate` lifts the five 2026-09-08 spread runs of case 01 to
`artifact_version` 7. With each `analysis-asvs.reports` directory copied
beside the lifted file, `score` reads it.

| Run | Correct | Accuracy | False prose requests |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 2026-09-08, r1 to r5 | 13 in each run | 72% to 76% | 1 in each run |
| 2026-10-10, `86bfffe2` | 13 of 17 | 76% | 3 |

The old runs predate ADR 0051, ADR 0075 and the critic fixes. A new run inside
their spread is a no-harm reading. A new run outside it needs #1476's "before"
arm to say what moved it.
