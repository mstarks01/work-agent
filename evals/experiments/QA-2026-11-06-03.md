## Answers

**What is hurting report quality?** Extraction copies a datasheet's claim about the whole product into one flow's control attribute. That stated control stops the spoofing lead for must-find 12/1.

**Which phase is responsible?** Extraction. The signed model and the case 12 corrections (row 4) hold the attribute as `unknown`.

**What is the proposed fix?** Rule 8 of "Reading what a source says": a claim about a whole product sets no control attribute on one element.

**What was changed, and what was measured?** E1, above. The candidate is kept.

**What did not work?** Nothing in this audit was refuted.

**What remains untested or blocked?** The effect on matched must-finds and on ASVS, and the cause of the Tier 1 difference between the arms.

**What was spent, and what remains?** $0.4724 of the $1.10 grant: $0.4693 for the ten sweeps and $0.0031 for the pre-flight. $0.6276 remains.

**What needs fresh model output, and which decision does it change?** This audit proposes no paid work. Each open question under "What remains untested or blocked?" needs fresh model output. The record holds no eight answers for them: the audit that asks writes them first.

**What should the next audit do first?** This audit names no first step.
