## Answers

**What is hurting report quality?** These archives show no lost lead, no lost place fact and no loss in the deterministic parse. Delivery of the other lane inputs is unverified (`-E4`). A missed must-find with a place or unled cause either had no candidate at its place, or had one that the lane did not take up. A wrong extracted fact was already wrong when `extract` emitted it.

**Which phase is responsible?**
- For the charged misses on the sweep: unresolved, and associated with the observed generation outcome (`-E4`, the maintainer's ruling of 2026-11-20). `-E1` assigned the four elevation-of-privilege rows to preparation and case 05 to analysis. The trace of `-E4` neither establishes nor refutes that.
- The four elevation-of-privilege rows need authority facts (#2054).
- For the wrong extracted facts: extraction.

**What was changed?**
- Per-attribute denominators in the replay (#2279).
- A delivery trace (#2282).
- The archived `fired_rules` in the place and unled decision (#2285).
- A stage and a certainty for each extracted fact (#2286).
- For `-E4`: the trace compares the four per-lane artifacts, and an agreeing lane reads `unverified`, never `equivalent` (#2315). The trace follows every charged miss and its matched peer, and reads each place fact at six stages (#2318). On the sweep, the preparation panel moves from `equivalent` to `unverified` for 5 cases. This is a measurement change, with no change to application output.

**What did not work?** Nothing was tried that lost. A code loss in extraction was refuted (`-E3`). A delivery loss was refuted for lead identities (`-E1`); `-E4` reads it as inconclusive, because the trace does not read every lane input or the facts the misses turn on.

**What remains untested or blocked?**
- Delivery of the shared lane keys, the prepared user turn, the prompt files and the case options.
- Scope that crosses subjects.
- Delivery in extraction mode.
- The live effect of any change on final findings.

**What should the next audit do first?** Read #2054's authority facts against the four elevation-of-privilege `unled` rows.
