# QA-2026-10-09-02: where does a round's capability box go?

#1570 made the first-run page show each group of a round in one box, so a
heading appears once in a round. The maintainer wants that result. This audit
measures what it cost, and which box order keeps it at no loss of STRIDE
findings.

All figures come from `run.py early-policies` over the archive at `5b9aa3a2`,
with `QA-2026-10-09-02/box-orders.patch` applied. `box_orders.py` is the script
that ran the policies, and `box-orders.json` is its output. The archive holds 186
STRIDE reports (2,796 conditional findings) and 31 ASVS reports (4,520 units)
on 11 cases. An owner answers from the top and stops after a number of choices.
I wrote both claims after the runs.

## The #1570 page order: refuted (E1)

Both frameworks selected:

| Choices | STRIDE, question turn order | STRIDE, #1570 | ASVS units, question turn order | ASVS units, #1570 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 5 | 1,009 | 52 | 173 | 864 |
| 10 | 1,147 | 103 | 902 | 1,489 |
| 20 | 1,293 | 1,293 | 1,806 | 1,806 |
| 30 | 1,865 | 1,760 | 1,930 | 2,027 |

Every capability question is in one group. The first capability question pulls
the whole capability box to the top of the round, so an owner who stops early
answers only ASVS questions. This is the loss of E29 again.

## Box orders: one is supported (E2)

Five orders show each group once in a round. Both frameworks selected, STRIDE
completed findings and ASVS settled units:

| Order | 5 choices | 10 | 20 | 30 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Question turn order | 1,009 / 173 | 1,147 / 902 | 1,293 / 1,806 | 1,865 / 1,930 |
| `boxes-in-turn-smaller` | 1,103 / 32 | 1,154 / 902 | 1,375 / 1,806 | 1,840 / 2,007 |
| `capabilities-at-middle` | 1,103 / 32 | 1,154 / 902 | 1,375 / 1,806 | 1,865 / 1,918 |
| `capability-boxes` | 919 / 472 | 1,154 / 917 | 1,293 / 1,806 | 1,865 / 1,930 |
| `boxes-in-turn` | 52 / 864 | 103 / 1,489 | 1,293 / 1,806 | 1,760 / 2,027 |

`capabilities-last` costs an ASVS-only job most of its early units: 77 against
864 at 5 choices.

`boxes-in-turn-smaller` ships as `answer_round.in_boxes` (ADR 0053). It names
no group and no framework, so a new package needs no edit. Its cost is ASVS
units at 5 choices, 32 against 173. The maintainer accepted that cost on
2026-10-09. With one framework selected, it completes as many findings as the
list order, or more.
