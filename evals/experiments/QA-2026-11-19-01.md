## Answers

**What is hurting report quality?** On the code at `42b63219`, the lanes lose most must-finds in their own drafts. The largest class is the verb: 13 of the 23 must-finds that the run misses. The attacker position is next, with 6. No must-find is lost at the critic, the fan-in or assembly.

**Which phase is responsible, how confidently, and what alternatives remain?** Analysis, for 20 of the 23 (verb 13, position 6, place 1). Preparation, for the 3 `unled` rows. The attribution is structural and comes from one run. For a `position` row, a corpus ruling on the reference's position is an alternative to a lane fix. For a `verb` row, a ruling by meaning can show that the draft states another attack (QA-2026-11-17-13-E2 found this for 8 of 9 archived rows).

**What is the proposed fix?** #2204's design, for the verb class. For the `unled` class, `-E5` keeps the lead rules C1a and C5b to batch with #2054 or #2204, and no paid run. This audit proposes none for the other classes.

**What was changed, and what was measured?** The audit changed nothing. It measured one paid run of 8 cases: 47 of 70 must-finds match, and the loss classes are in the tables below.

**What did not work?** The audit tested no change.

**What was spent, and what remains?** $2.5108 of the $3.00 grant, by `node_charges`. $0.4892 remains.

**What remains untested or blocked?**
- The spread. This is one run.
- The configuration production runs. The run set `ANALYSIS_ASSERTIONS=false` (`-E3`).
- The live effect of the lead rules C1a and C5b (`-E5`). The maintainer decided on no paid run for them alone.
- The 5 cases of 01 to 13 that the $3 grant did not reach: 02, 03, 04, 05 and 07 (33 must-finds).
- A ruling by meaning on the 13 verb rows and the 6 position rows.
- #2204's design against the verb rows.

**What needs fresh model output, and which decision does it change?** A test of #2204's design decides whether the verb-class design is kept. The spread of this run decides whether a later reading against 47/70 can show a change. Only new calls measure either. The audit that asks for that spend writes the eight answers of `references/experiment-protocol.md` first.

**What should the next audit do first?** Rule the 13 must-find verb rows by meaning, from the drafts in the `.reports` folders. Then test #2204's design against the rows that read `same`. Six of the 13 references (09/4, 10/0, 10/17, 12/17, 13/16, 13/19) are also among the 9 verb rows of QA-2026-11-17-13-E2. That audit ruled 8 of its 9 `different`.
