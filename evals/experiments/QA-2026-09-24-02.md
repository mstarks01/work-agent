## Answers

**What is hurting report quality?** `place` is the largest STRIDE loss class
on every Baseline. In the three runs that captured lane material, a `place`
loss is a lane that **was** shown the reference's place and drafted nothing
there. The lead arrived.

**Which phase is responsible?** Analysis, for these rows. Preparation delivered
the lead: an exact candidate, the elements in the evidence catalog, and the
lane's scope line. `QA-2026-09-22-02-E6` showed that `place` does not imply this
in general; the captured material shows it holds for every must-find row here.

**What is the proposed fix?** None yet. `QA-2026-09-22-02-E4` refuted a blanket
"draft at every led place": the lanes drafted at about half their led places on
6bff717 too (information disclosure 112 led, 58 drafted, 56 listed), so drafting
everywhere doubles output against a reference that lists about as many. A fix
has to be selective, and nothing here says yet which leads to take.

**What did the lanes do instead?** On case 07 the spoofing lane drafted one
claim from five leads, about the shared build token, where the reference lists
three per-flow impersonations. The lane wrote one claim per mechanism rather
than one per place. Two cases cannot say whether that is the pattern.

**What did not work?** Nothing was tried. E2 is inconclusive by sample size.

**What remains untested or blocked?** E3: whether the pass-by is systematic or
run-to-run variance. `run.py lane-replay` answers it for one call per repeat.

**What was spent?** $0.

**What should the next audit do first?** Read E1 and E2 again over the lane
material of the #1177 sweep, which captures 36 runs at no extra cost.

**The spoofing matches are false by meaning.** Both say a fake image registry
impersonates itself to store servers, the opposite direction to the reference.
The identity rule is undirected by decision (ADR 0031), so it matches them.
That is the decision's documented limit, not a new defect, and it is why a
structural match is read for meaning before it counts.

**The tampering lane writes the reference's threat in 6 of 6 repeats, one
place over.** "Unverified registry push access could replace a deployed
container image" cites the push path (build runner to registry), where the
attacker acts. The reference cites registry to store server, where the harm
lands. This is a disagreement about which place a planted-image finding
belongs at, not an omission, and it is the owner's to rule on.

**Next.** The case-01 row needs nothing: the lane finds it one time in three.
The two case-07 rows need the owner's rulings before any prompt change: which
place a planted-image finding belongs at, and whether "a store server
impersonated to the registry" is a finding the lane should be led to write

## The fan-in one-hop bound, priced offline (E7, $0)

The fan-in removes a cited element more than one hop from the places a claim's
grounds name (#441). The `fan-in` loss cause (#1171) found it cutting the
reference's element in 18 misses across four Baselines. Because the rule is
deterministic, its price is exact: fan-in re-run over the archived lane
proposals of 52 case-runs, the kept drafts carrying their archived critic
verdict, and re-scored.

| | V0 one hop (shipped) | V1 two hops | V2 no bound |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Must-finds matched | 201 | **206** | 207 |
| All matches | 381 | 394 | 395 |
| Unlisted claims | 86 | 82 | 82 |
| Mean element_jaccard over matches | 0.420 | 0.413 | 0.413 |
| Mean elements per claim | 2.71 | 2.81 | 2.82 |

**Read for meaning, V1's 15 changed matches:** all 5 must-find gains are the
reference's finding; of the 14 gains in total, 12 are correct and 2 are false
(case 01 ref 9, case 13 ref 3); the one match lost (case 05 ref 13) was itself
doubtful. Seven of the twelve are repudiation claims: the store that keeps the
record sits two hops from the flow the grounds cite.

**Caveats.** V0 reproduced the archive in 45 of 52 case-runs; the other 7 hold
older flow-ID spellings, unrelated to the bound. A kept draft keeps its
archived critic verdict, and a critic shown the wider draft could rule
differently. The gain, about 1.2 points of must-find coverage, is below what a
paid sweep can resolve, but it does not need one: the replay is deterministic.

**Decision owed.** Widening the bound reverses part of #441, whose purpose was
element precision, so adopting V1 is the maintainer's decision.
