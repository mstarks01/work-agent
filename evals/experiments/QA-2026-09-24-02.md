## Answers

**What is hurting report quality?** `place` is the largest STRIDE loss class
on every Baseline. In the three runs that captured lane material, a `place`
loss is a lane that **was** shown the reference's place and drafted nothing
there. The lead arrived.

**Which phase is responsible?** Analysis, for these rows. Preparation delivered
the lead: an exact candidate, the elements in the evidence catalog, and the
lane's scope line. `QA-2026-09-22-02-E6` showed that `place` does not imply this
in general; the captured material shows it holds for every must-find row here.

**What is the proposed fix?** None yet. `QA-2026-09-22-02-E4` refuted a blanket
"draft at every led place": the lanes drafted at about half their led places on
6bff717 too (information disclosure 112 led, 58 drafted, 56 listed), so drafting
everywhere doubles output against a reference that lists about as many. A fix
has to be selective, and nothing here says yet which leads to take.

**What did the lanes do instead?** On case 07 the spoofing lane drafted one
claim from five leads, about the shared build token, where the reference lists
three per-flow impersonations. The lane wrote one claim per mechanism rather
than one per place. Two cases cannot say whether that is the pattern.

**What did not work?** Nothing was tried. E2 is inconclusive by sample size.

**What remains untested or blocked?** E3: whether the pass-by is systematic or
run-to-run variance. `run.py lane-replay` answers it for one call per repeat.

**What was spent?** $0.

**What should the next audit do first?** Read E1 and E2 again over the lane
material of the #1177 sweep, which captures 36 runs at no extra cost.
