## Answers

**What is hurting report quality?** On the 2026-10-24 sweep, 9 must-finds of 100 have a draft at the reference's place under another verb. In 8 of the 9, the maintainer ruled the draft a different attack. The lane wrote a real finding at that place, but with another attacker or access path. In 6 of those 8 the lane wrote an on-path attacker (4) or a storage copy (2), where the reference names a party that holds a legitimate path.

**Which phase is responsible?** Analysis. The lane received the place (QA-2026-11-17-12-E1 traced delivery), and it wrote at it.

**What was changed?** #2280 fixed the loss instrument. It does not change a finding.

**What did not work?** Nothing was tried that lost. `-E2` refutes the reading that these rows are the reference's attack under another verb.

**What remains untested or blocked?** Whether any intervention makes the lane write the reference's mechanism. That needs fresh model output, and #2204 owns that experiment.

**What should the next audit do first?** Price the design below as part of #2204's Phase 2, against the controls named there.

## A design for #2204 (untested intervention)

This is a hypothesis. No run tested it, and this audit authorises none.

- **Hypothesis.** Where a source states a party that holds a legitimate path to a place (a store writer, the user's own client, a party that drives a tool, a holder of a session), the lane writes an on-path or storage-copy attacker in place of that party. A lane asked to account for each supported mechanism at a led place, separately, writes the holder's attack as well.
- **Seam.** The lane and lead seam of #2204's Phase 2: a bounded list of mechanisms for each led place, built from the System Model and the evidence catalog, never from a reference.
- **Target rows.** The 6 on-path and storage-copy rows: 02/3, 03/8, 06/3, 09/4, 10/17, 13/19. The other 2 different rows (12/17, 13/16) are a second signature, and they are read but not targeted.
- **Expected failure signature.** A draft at the target place states an on-path or storage-copy attacker, and no draft states the holder of the legitimate path.
- **Controls.** The 21 matched must-finds, the 6 rows ruled "same" in `-E1`, and each target row's present draft. A gain must not replace a mechanism that already works: the on-path or storage-copy draft at a target place is a valid finding, and it must stay.
- **Falsifier.** Over paired repeated runs on the sweep's model, the treatment recovers fewer than 2 more of the 6 targets than the control, judged by meaning and blind to the arm. It is also falsified if it loses any of the 21 controls or any of the 6 "same" mechanisms in more than one pass.
- **Output burden.** Proposals for each lane rise by no more than 50%. QA-2026-09-26-01-E2 rose about 75% for its gain. Each new finding that matches no reference is labelled before any count of unsupported findings is stated.
- **Prior refutations.** QA-2026-10-27-01-E2 ordered the parties in a paragraph of the output contract. On terra the treatment matched 4 of 28 targets against 6 of 28, and it lost the firmware plant at 02/3. QA-2026-11-03-01-E3 refuted a grant-holder contrast exemplar. This design differs in two ways: it asks for one account for each mechanism at a led place, not an order of parties, and it must run on the sweep's model, not on terra.
- **Regressions to watch.** A higher proposal count, duplicate findings at one place, and an invented holder where the source states none.

## Reproduction

Every command runs offline, with `ANALYSIS_OFFLINE=1`, from a checkout at `46b08868`. `evals/runs/` is not in git. `score.sh` holds absolute paths of the maintainer's machine. `rows.py` takes each path as an argument, and it imports `evals.harness` from the checkout, so it needs `PYTHONPATH=.`.

```bash
bash evals/experiments/QA-2026-11-17-13/score.sh <checkout> <scored dir>
PYTHONPATH=. uv run python evals/experiments/QA-2026-11-17-13/rows.py <scored dir> \
  evals/runs/20261024T-sonnet-medium-sweep evals/corpus evals/experiments/QA-2026-11-17-13
```

For `-E1`, run `score.sh` once from a checkout at `6de96fa6` and once at `d4bfc1af`. Then run `uv run python -m evals.harness.run score <baseline>/mstarks01-*.json --out <out>.json` for each Baseline, at both commits.
