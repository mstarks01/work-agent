# Quality audit `QA-2026-10-01-01`

**Coordinates.** Commit `37f20cdc`, framework `stride`, scope the analysis
phase, mode audit and fix, budget $1.30, spent $1.264. The audit answers the
offline findings of #1295.

**Status.** Complete. Four rows: E1 and E3 supported, E2 refuted and
superseded by E3, E4 supported.

## E1: a duplicate rejection can drop a distinct remedy

The four Baselines hold 3 duplicate rejections against 867 kept claims. Two of
the three dropped a draft whose remedy differs from its kept partner's (08 E-02,
13 S-03). Both drafts of a pair share one claim identity, so no must-find is
lost. The rate is too low to change the claim identity rule, and keeping both
drafts would put two kept claims on one identity.

## E2 and E3: the source text can change a critic verdict

E2 read every draft for a premise its own source defeats, and found none. It
read one direction only. E3 read all 34 rejections beside their sources. One
rejection dropped a must-find whose premise the model held in `notes` and the
source states plainly (6bff717 07 T-01). Two more turn on a source sentence the
model does not carry.

## E4: the critic with and without the sources

One `run.py critic-replay` call for each arm, over the 13 cases of Baseline
6bff717. The arms agree on 179 of 195 verdicts. Today's critic prompt keeps
07 T-01 without the sources. With the sources, five drafts whose premise the
source states plainly move from `needs-info` to `confirmed`. Case 12 keeps all
16 verdicts. A call costs about 2% more. ADR 0057 records the decision.
