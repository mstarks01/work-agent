## Proposal for paid work (approved, blocked)

The grant of 2026-11-06 approves this at $2. The pre-flight call (arm B, case
02) failed with `invalid_request_error`: "Your credit balance is too low to
access the Anthropic API" (request `req_011Cgky2LxquZ9eEmH1NVhhu`). Another
model is not a substitute, because the archived sweep ran on Sonnet 5. The
script is `replay.sh` in the audit's session scratchpad and is not kept.

1. **Question.** Does the control step move the critic's verdicts on drafts that carry no `controls`? If yes, render step 3 only when a draft carries `controls`. If no, keep the prompt as it is.
2. **Offline checks.** E1 and the word count above.
3. **Ledger.** No row tests #2171. E3 and E5 of QA-2026-10-08-02 tested another prompt edit; `prompts/critic.md` changed since, so they are stale.
4. **Why no free check answers it.** The question is what the model rules. A replay of an archived verdict is not new model output.
5. **Stage and cases.** The critic only, by `critic-replay`, on the 13 STRIDE cases of `20261024T-sonnet-medium-sweep` (the newest full sweep), Sonnet 5, as archived.
6. **Metric and plan.** The share of drafts whose verdict differs from the archived one. Arm A: one pass at `d51830a1` in a worktree (the old prompt, for the band). Arm B: one pass at `HEAD`. The effect is real if B differs from the archive by more than A does, and by more than 5% of drafts.
7. **Spend.** About $2.00 for 26 critic calls, estimated from E3 ($0.83 for 13 calls). A retry of one case is about $0.08.
8. **If null.** Record it and keep the prompt. If adverse, propose the conditional step. If inconclusive, stop; do not add passes without a new ask.
