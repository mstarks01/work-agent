# QA-2026-10-31-01: the early order counts the rules that read a fact

Scope: package 5 of #2029, an offline comparison of three orders of the early
question list. Mode offline, no grant, $0 spent.

**Status: closed.** E3 is the default since #2041 (ADR 0115).

## What was compared

Before #2041 the early list scored a field question as the prior's rate times
one plus every candidate that names the element. The floor read that score,
and the list ranked by it per choice. Each order below counts only the
candidates whose rule reads the fact (`early_questions.reads`).

`run.py early-policies` read each order over the Baselines and the first ASVS
sweep: 11 cases with reports of both frameworks, every question answered "I
don't know", at budgets of 5 to 60 choices.

| Row | Order | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| E1 | a question kind counts every candidate; the floor reads the new value | refuted: STRIDE alone 260 against 383 findings at 10 choices |
| E2 | a question kind counts none; the floor reads the new value | refuted: every question kind fell under the floor and left the list |
| E3 | a question kind counts none; the floor reads the shipped score | supported: 429 against 383 at 10 choices, 510 against 446 at 15, same questions asked |

## Artifacts

- `QA-2026-10-31-01/early-policies.json`: E3, run at 0a2fcb5f from a clean
  tree. `element-count` is the order before #2041.
- `QA-2026-10-31-01/variants.json`: E1 and E2, run on the branch of #2041.
- `QA-2026-10-31-01/variants.py`: E1 and E2 against 0a2fcb5f. They reproduce

The counts are dependency sets on archived reports, not findings that an
answer changes. The live effect is unmeasured.
