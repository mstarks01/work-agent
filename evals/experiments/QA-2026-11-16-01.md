# QA-2026-11-16-01: extraction of the capacity-limits parts

Scope: phase 2 of #2054 (ADR 0117), the first predicate group. The assertion
pass extracts `capacity-limits-rate`, `-size`, `-concurrency` and `-quota`
since #2266. The mode is implement. Two grants give $1.50, and the runs used
$0.8061.

**Status: closed.** The gate passed, and #2266 promoted the group.

## What the rows say

- E1: the model wrote no `not applicable` value, and no value on the 7 silent
  rows. Its 7 misses were `rescoped` rows. The six `*.replay.json` files are
  the scorer output.
- E2: the candidate lost case 17's must-find 7, many large uploads. The verb
  rule named `flood` for many items and `exhaust-resources` for one costly
  item, and it named no verb for many large items. #2265 gave that case to
  `flood`.
- E3: G3 ran again on the fixed rule. Case 17's must-find 7 matched in 2 of 3
  runs on each arm.
- G3 ran `lane-replay --prepare-today signed`. The lane read the signed
  capacity rows, not the extracted rows. No run measured the pass and the lane
  together.

`lanes.tsv` lists each lane run with its commit, its spend and the must-finds
it matched. The pre-flight lane run kept no log, so its two figures are derived
from the totals, and the table says so.

## After the runs

A read of #2266 found that the model can write "no limit" as `absent` or as
`no`. The resolver now writes an extracted `absent` of a capacity part as `no`,
through `missing_value`. That change has no live measurement: the runs above
wrote no `absent` capacity row.

The fix is verified by the listed offline evidence; its live quality effect
remains unmeasured.
