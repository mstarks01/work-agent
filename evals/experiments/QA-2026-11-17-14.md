## Answers

**What is hurting report quality?** One deterministic step could weaken evidence: the quote repair kept a span that stated the opposite fact. It is fixed, and no archived report carried such a span. The other findings are measurement gaps: empty rates that read as zero, and a critic audit with no labelled unsupported drafts.

**Which phase is responsible?** Fan-in for `-E3`. The critic instrument for `-E1`, `-E2` and `-E4`. `-E5` found no phase that promotes an open fact.

**What was changed?**
- The critic panel and empty critic-yield rates (#2283).
- Every empty rate reads unavailable (#2288). `ARTIFACT_VERSION` stays 7 by the maintainer's ruling.
- The quote repair refuses a moved span (#2297).
- Evidence-state fixtures with valid neighbours (#2299).

**What did not work?** Nothing was tried that lost.

**What remains untested or blocked?**
- The rate at which the critic keeps an unsupported draft. No label for one exists, and no new labels are made by decision.
- R2, ruled. Where a projection is not written into the graph, a settled row beside an opposite graph value stayed citable, and the report named the contradiction. This audit recommended to keep it citable and pinned, because a question would hide a fact the source states. The maintainer ruled the other way on 2026-11-18: the row is an open question, like any other conflict. #2302 applies the ruling (`PROJECTION_VERSION` 7, `POPULATION_VERSION` 2). Four follow-ups of #2302 are in progress and not done: the graph side of a contradiction, the route of an answer to a contradicted row, compatible rows beside an opposite graph value, and a disputed pair under a refused projection.
- The critic replay's agreement count, ruled. It counted a returned or omitted draft, and an unruled one the replay ruled on, as a disagreement. This audit recommended to count those drafts apart, as "no archived verdict". The maintainer ruled on 2026-11-18 that the count leaves a draft with no archived verdict out of M. #2303 applies the ruling.
- The `catalog_coverage` count, ruled. It counts a contradicted attribute as settled. The maintainer ruled on 2026-11-18 that it counts the attribute as open. The change is in progress and not done.
- The `critic_yield.CriticPopulations` denominators, ruled. They count returned and omitted drafts. The maintainer ruled on 2026-11-18 that the rates leave a returned, omitted or unruled draft out of the denominators and count it apart. The change is in progress and not done.
- Ruled unchanged on 2026-11-18: the lane gloss of an open row, `prompts/critic.md` line 33 and the webapp label.
- Run-to-run variation: each group is one run.

**What should the next audit do first?** Read the critic panel again after the `CriticPopulations` change merges, because the panel's rates change with their denominators.
