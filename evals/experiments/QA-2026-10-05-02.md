writes the threat at a neighbouring element (place and unled, 18 must-finds),
or files it under another verb (10 must-finds).

**Which phase is responsible?** Analysis, for most of the place class and all
of the verb class. Preparation owns at most the unled rows. The ledger records
this split in QA-2026-09-22-02-E2.

**What is the proposed fix?** No new fix. Earlier audits refuted both rule
families, every verb equivalence, and the "lane writes nonsense" reading.

**What was changed, and what was measured?** No code changed. E1 and E2
measured the two `stale` answers that the identity work could move.

**What did not work?** E1 and E2 are both refuted.

**What remains untested or blocked?** Pattern A waits on the blind ballot
(#1239 item 1). The vote on the lane-closing findings waits on the maintainer
(#1239 item 3).

**What was spent, and what remains?** $0 spent. Budget $0.

**What needs fresh model output, and which decision does it change?** A
STRIDE sweep at this revision. It is the only way to measure the tree after
ADR 0030 and ADRs 0059 to 0062, and it gives a Baseline on the corpus that
stands. It is the open-work item "full 15-case sweep with the lane closing"
in `docs/agents/report-quality.md`, about $5.50. This audit does not ask for
it again: the user deferred it, and the only change since is code whose
effect E1 and E2 show to be neutral on the archive.

**What should the next audit do first?** #1239 item 1 is ruled (E3) and
the store-readers rule is merged. #1239 item 3 is voted (E4): the cost of
ADR 0030 now waits on answers to the open facts. Two leads stay open: the
compromised-reader references 05/6 and 05/7, and one attack filed in two
lanes at one place. E5 tested ADR 0060's grounded controls as the field that
folds the second one, and it does not.
