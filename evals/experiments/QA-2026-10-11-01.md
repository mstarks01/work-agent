# QA-2026-10-11-01: does the quote ladder read a transcript?

For [#87](https://github.com/mstarks01/work-agent/issues/87) measurement 2 on
[#64](https://github.com/mstarks01/work-agent/issues/64). The quote
normalization ladder (`src/analysis_service/grounding.py`, called from
`join_drafts`) was measured on description-only sources. Two shapes a
transcript adds rested on an argument: a quote that keeps a speaker label, and
a quote that spans two turns. Case 16 (`16-library-lending-interview`, #1589)
is the first transcript case, so #64 carried the obligation to report the
ladder's false-rejection rate on that case alone.

## The runs

Two paid runs on 2026-10-10 at `fe8e309c` (the #1589 merge commit, clean
tree). STRIDE only, `openrouter/openai/gpt-5.6-luna-pro` on every tier,
`ANALYSIS_ASSERTIONS=false`. No estimate was recorded before the runs. The
artifacts are in `QA-2026-10-11-01/`.

## E1: the false-rejection rate, supported (0 of 24)

The `grounds` block of each artifact holds the counts. Each refusal was walked
back to its quote through the `unverified` indices.

| | analysis | end to end |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Quote grounds | 24 | 32 |
| Refused by the ladder | 2 | 1 |
| Of those, false rejections | 0 | 0 |
| Rewritten by the repair rung | 1 | 1 |
| Quotes that keep a speaker label (`Priya:`) | 4, all verified | 6, all verified |
| Quotes that span two turns | 0 | 0 |

The analysis run's unverified rate is 8.3% (2 of 24), against 2.0% and 3.4% in
the two description-only sweeps #87 reports. Both refusals are true:

- `I-06` quotes "The API is the only thing that reads or writes it." and names
  the note as its source. The sentence is in the transcript, line 19, and not
  in the note. The ladder checks the source a ground names, so the refusal is
  correct. The defect is a wrong source label, not a manufactured sentence.
  The end-to-end run refused the same quote under the same label (`I-05`).
- `I-07` quotes two sentences from Priya's turn at line 15 with the sentence
  between them cut out and no `…` mark. The transcript never holds the quote
  as written. This is the unmarked cut the ladder exists to refuse.

So the ladder reads a transcript: every speaker-labelled quote verified, and
the two refusals are the two quotes a reader would refuse. The turn-spanning
shape is still unmeasured, because no quote in either run spans a turn.

The 8.3% rate is 2 refusals over a denominator of 24. One case cannot place
the transcript rate inside or outside the 2-3% band.
