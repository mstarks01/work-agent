# QA-2026-10-20-01: Claude on the strong tier, effort and time

Scope `analysis`, mode `audit`, four grants, $19.50 in total; $17.97 spent,
priced from token counts at list rates, because the `anthropic` row reports no
charge. Every run used commit `9983b527`. The artifacts are under
`evals/runs/20261020T-claude-tiers/`, which git does not track.

These runs advance no open `needs-sweep` issue (#1476, #1413, #744, #661), and
the maintainer did not waive that rule for them. They answered a question from
the first-run app: why a report on Claude failed with timeouts.

## The question

With base `claude-haiku-4-5` and strong `claude-sonnet-5` on the `anthropic`
row, a first-run report failed: lane calls passed the 300 s request timeout,
and the job passed its 900 s deadline. Is that a runaway output or a slow
answer, and which strong model and effort fit the timeout?

## Results

E1 to E3 are end-to-end runs of case 01, STRIDE only, with a 30-minute request
timeout and one attempt. Each run made its own extraction on Haiku 4.5.

| | Sonnet 5 `high` (E1) | Sonnet 5 `medium` (E2) | Opus 5.5 `medium` (E3) |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| cost | $2.23 | $1.56 | $1.78 |
| slowest lane | 272 s | 157 s | 54 s |
| critic | 430 s | 283 s | 87 s |
| lane output, six lanes | 118,807 | 63,103 | 30,310 |
| must-finds | 7 of 8 | 6 of 8 | 4 of 8 |

E1 refutes the runaway: no node reached the 64,000-token cap. Sonnet 5 writes
at about terra's speed, but each lane writes 4 to 7 times more, mostly thinking.
E1 cost more than the $2 upper figure stated before the run; the harness
estimate, $0.31, came from terra token counts.

E4 removes the extraction: `lane-replay --prepare-today none`, six lanes, three
passes an arm. Seven must-finds are reachable by the lanes.

| arm | must-finds per pass | slowest lane | thinking a pass |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: |
| Opus 5.5 `high` | 7, 7, 7 | 72-100 s | 6K-16K |
| Sonnet 5 `high` | 6, 7, 7 | 250-341 s | 41K-117K |
| Sonnet 5 `medium` | 5, 6, 5 | 91-131 s | 14K-29K |

Within Sonnet 5, more effort found more must-finds. Across models, thinking
volume did not rank them: Opus 5.5 at `high` thought about a tenth as much as
Sonnet 5 at `high` and found as many or more.

## What follows

- The shipped 300 s timeout fails Sonnet 5 at `high` on this pipeline.
- Opus 5.5 at `high` is the Claude strong tier these runs support. Its critic
  at `high` is unmeasured.
- Never price a Claude run from terra token counts.

The quality figures are one case. Their live effect on the corpus remains
unmeasured.
