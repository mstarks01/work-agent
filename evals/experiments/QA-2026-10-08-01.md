# QA-2026-10-08-01: early-round workload at the current catalog

Part of [#1542](https://github.com/mstarks01/work-agent/issues/1542), work
package B: refresh the offline workload measurement for the expanded
capability catalog, per framework and per band, with several answerers.

## Claim

ADR 0053's workload figures still hold: a STRIDE pause takes a median of 6
rounds, and an ASVS pause takes 3 rounds and asks 26 yes/no questions.

## Result: refuted (E1)

`run.py early-rounds` over the 202 archived reports with a blessed model, at
the revision the ledger row names. Each replay now records the stop's summary
(ADR 0068). The artifact is `QA-2026-10-08-01/early-rounds.json`.

| Selection / answerer | Rounds (median) | Asked (median) | Under the floor at the stop | Held back at the stop | Unknown at the stop, level 1 / level 2 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| STRIDE, answers given | 6 | 30 | 55 | 2 | — |
| STRIDE, skips everything | 7 | 32 | 55 | 0 | — |
| ASVS level 2, every capability yes | 3 | 34 | 71 | 28 | 10% / 60% |
| ASVS level 2, every capability no | 3 | 34 | 43 | 12 | 4% / 34% |
| ASVS level 2, skips everything | 6 | 63 | 71 | 0 | 90% / 83% |
| Both frameworks, capability yes | 6 | 54 | 122 | 28 | 10% / 60% |

STRIDE still takes 6 rounds. ASVS still takes 3 rounds, but it asks 34
questions, not 26: the parts asked beside their parents take no place under the
limit of 30. The figure in ADR 0053 is historical.

A full ASVS pause does not settle applicability. With every capability
answered "yes", a median of 60% of level 2 requirements is still unknown at the
stop, because the limit of 30 holds 28 more capability questions back and 71
sit under the floor. These are applicability units, not findings, and the
replays read extracted models with no catalog. The summary now reports these
numbers to the owner, and the held-back and below-floor questions take an
answer. The limits were not changed: no measurement here says what a larger
limit costs an owner.
