# QA-2026-10-31-02: do the lanes use early answers to question kinds?

Scope: a paid pre-flight for #2029. Question kinds hold 84% of the choices of
the web app example's pause, and deferring them to the report's follow-up
would cut the pause by about 80% (QA-2026-10-31-01-E2). The question was
whether the lanes use an early answer to a question kind. Mode audit, one
grant of $1.00, $0.6741 spent, 18 of 18 calls.

**Status: closed.** E1 is refuted: the lanes use the answers.

## E1

Lane replay of the six STRIDE lanes of case 01, from the archived material of
`evals/runs/20261021T-1413-tool-vs-native/native-r1`, on `gpt-5.6-terra`
through OpenRouter, one pass for each arm.

| Arm | Drafts | Drafts that quote an answer |
| --- | --- | --- |
| control, no answers | 26 | 0 |
| every facet "yes" | 18 | 12 |
| every facet "no" | 33 | 22 |

The answers are fixed, not true, so this shows that the lanes react to early
answers, not that the reports improve. One pass an arm leaves the spread
unmeasured; the quoted answers are direct evidence of use.
