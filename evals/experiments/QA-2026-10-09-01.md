# QA-2026-10-09-01: does one package starve another in a joint pause?

For [#1562](https://github.com/mstarks01/work-agent/issues/1562), which carries
#1542 Package F: measure starvation and early coverage separately for each
package.

## Claim

When STRIDE and ASVS share the rounds, the limits starve neither package. On
the same reports, each package asks a median within 2 questions of what it asks
alone, and its median held back rises by 2 or fewer.

## Result: refuted (E1)

`run.py early-rounds` over 254 archived reports with a blessed model, at the
revision the ledger row names. 223 reports select STRIDE and 31 select ASVS (17
at level 2, 14 at level 1). Each report is replayed alone and again with both
packages, with its own options kept. The artifact is
`QA-2026-10-09-01/early-rounds.json`.

Paired by report, with every capability answered "yes":

| Package | Asked, alone / joint (median) | Held back at the stop | Under the floor at the stop | Reports that lose 3 or more |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| STRIDE (223) | 30 / 27 | 2 / 4 | 55 / 54 | 55 ask 3 to 5 fewer; 58 hold back 3 to 5 more |
| ASVS (31) | 34 / 35 | 28 / 28 | 71 / 70 | none |

STRIDE loses places when ASVS shares the rounds. The joint median asked falls
by 3, which is more than the declared bound of 2. The paired median change is
0: a quarter of the STRIDE reports lose 3 to 5 questions, and the other reports
lose 2 or fewer. ASVS loses nothing: it asks the same or more, and its held-back
count does not change in any pair.

This measures counts of questions, not findings. No measurement here says
whether the STRIDE questions that a joint pause holds back change a report.

## Corrections to QA-2026-10-08-01

- Its table labels the ASVS rows "level 2". 14 of the 31 ASVS reports are at
  level 1, and each alone replay used the report's own level.
- Its "both frameworks" row replayed each ASVS level 1 report at level 2, because
  the joint replay took the options of the first archived job. #1571 fixed the
  replay. The figures in that row are not like for like with the alone rows.
