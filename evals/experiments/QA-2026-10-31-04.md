# QA-2026-10-31-04: does a node's shape state its element type?

Scope: the follow-up to QA-2026-10-31-03-E1. Mode implement, one grant of
$1.00, $0.8882 metered at list price (an upper bound).

**Status: closed.** E1 is refuted by the keep rule: the shape sentence fixes
the clean diagrams and harms the drawn ones, so the candidate is not merged.

## Before the experiment

1. **The observed failure.** In QA-2026-10-31-03-E1 the diagram arm lost 7.75
   elements a sweep. Most of the loss is a wrong element type and the flows
   that end at that element: case 10's CDN and case 02's sensor node typed as
   a Process, case 09's catalogue spreadsheet as an External Entity, case 03's
   data analyst. Artifacts: `evals/emissions/20261031T-diagram-input/diagram-r*`.
2. **The suspected mechanism.** The diagram register says "The shape of a node
   states no attribute", while each node's shape in the export named the
   blessed type. A competing explanation: the model types these elements from
   their names whatever the register says.
3. **The change.** One register sentence, on branch
   `candidate/diagram-shape-states-type` (`98ac260b`): "The shape of a node can
   state its type: `id[text]` is an external entity, `id(text)` a process and
   `id[(text)]` a data store. The shape states no other attribute." Only a
   `diagram` source's header changes. No other prompt byte changes.
4. **The outcome.** Elements lost per sweep, as QA-2026-10-31-03 defines it:
   the sum of `mistyped`, `misattached`, `endpoint_unaligned`, `omitted`,
   `ambiguous` and `unresolved` from `run.py replay` over `evals/corpus`. The
   ceiling on the clean set is the 2.75 mistyped elements a sweep and the 4.0
   unaligned flows they cause.
5. **The falsifier.** On the clean set, the candidate's mean elements lost is
   not lower than the before arm's mean by at least half of the before arm's
   standard deviation.
6. **The smallest experiment.** Two diagram sets:
   - **clean:** `evals/diagrams/<case>.mmd`, the export of each blessed model.
     The before side is the archived `diagram-r1` to `diagram-r4` of
     QA-2026-10-31-03 at `ab7dc98d`. No prompt byte differs between
     `ab7dc98d` and `main` at `47e995b4`, where the candidate branch starts.
     The after side is four fresh sweeps at the candidate.
   - **drawn:** `QA-2026-10-31-04/drawn/<case>.mmd`, a diagram of each case
     drawn from `source.md` alone, by an agent that did not open the blessed
     model, in the loose way a person draws. No person has reviewed them.
     Three sweeps at `main` and three at the candidate.
   All 16 cases, `gpt-5.6-luna` through OpenRouter, three cases in flight,
   the arms in turn. `QA-2026-10-31-04/build_diagram_corpus.py` writes each corpus.
   Each arm is replayed with `run.py replay` over `evals/corpus`.
7. **Cost and acceptance.** The route has no price table, so the spend is
   metered from each artifact's tokens at OpenRouter's list price, an upper
   bound. A diagram sweep read about $0.085 in QA-2026-10-31-03, so ten sweeps
   and a pre-flight read about $0.86. A sweep that would take the spend past
   $0.95 does not start.

## The gate

| Set | Result | Reading |
| --- | --- | --- |
| clean | after mean ≤ before mean − 0.5 before sd | supported: the shape sentence recovers typed elements |
| clean | otherwise | refuted |
| drawn | after mean > before mean + 0.5 before sd | harm: the sentence costs a loosely drawn diagram more than it gains |
| either | an arm short of its sweeps | inconclusive |

**The decision.** Keep the candidate only if the clean set reads supported and
the drawn set reads no harm. Otherwise the branch is not merged, and the row
keeps the reason.

**Read, not gated:** `mistyped` alone, `endpoint_unaligned`, `placeholder`,
wrong facts on paired elements, the cases the gate refused, and the drawn
set's figures beside the clean set's, which say what loose drawing costs.

## E1 result

Elements lost per sweep, from `run.py replay` over `evals/corpus`:

| Set | Before | After (candidate) | Gate |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| clean | 9, 5, 11, 6 (mean 7.75, sd 2.75) | 1, 0, 1, 1 (mean 0.75) | supported: line 6.4 |
| drawn | 75, 75, 81 (mean 77.0, sd 3.46) | 132, 142, 131 (mean 135.0) | harm: line 78.7 |

Read, not gated (per sweep):

| Figure | clean before | clean after | drawn before | drawn after |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `mistyped` | 2.75 | 0.0 | 1.3 | 26.0 |
| `endpoint_unaligned` | 4.0 | 0.0 | 36.3 | 71.0 |
| `omitted` | 0.25 | 0.0 | 17.0 | 11.7 |
| `unresolved` | 0.75 | 0.75 | 21.7 | 26.3 |
| `placeholder` | 0.0 | 0.0 | 15.0 | 15.0 |
| sweeps with a refused case | 2 of 4 | 0 of 4 | 1 of 3 | 3 of 3 |

- **On the clean set the sentence works.** Every mistyped element and every
  flow it cost came back.
- **On the drawn set it does harm.** The drawings use plain and rounded boxes
  loosely; case 10 draws its people as rounded boxes. The sentence turns those
  shapes into types, and mistyped elements rise from 1.3 to 26.0 a sweep.
- **The decision.** By the declared keep rule the candidate is not kept.
  `candidate/diagram-shape-states-type` (`98ac260b`) is not merged, and the
  register keeps "The shape of a node states no attribute."
- **The drawn set's level is not an extraction figure.** Its 77.0 elements
  lost a sweep, against 14.75 for the description in QA-2026-10-31-03, mixes
  what each drawing left out, labels the aligner cannot pair, and reading
  errors. It compares the two arms with each other. The drawings are an
  agent's, from `source.md` alone, and no person has reviewed them. The case
  14 and 15 drawings hold three arrows their descriptions imply but do not
  state.
- **Next candidates.** Two narrower changes could keep the clean gain: a
  sentence that names only the cylinder as a data store, or an export that
  states its shape convention in the file, so the sentence applies only where
  a diagram says it follows the convention. Each is a new experiment with its
  own grant.
