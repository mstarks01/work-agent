# QA-2026-11-02-03: where case 05 loses the compromised-reader threat

Scope: item 4 of #2105. Case 05 STRIDE references 6 and 7, by zero-based index
into today's `evals/corpus/05-cookbook-queue-webapp/claims/stride.json`. Mode
diagnose. Paid inference: $0.

## The references

| Ref | Claim | Store | Reader |
|---|---|---|---|
| 6 | An attacker who compromises the background worker reads the database credentials from its config store. | `store:worker-config` | `process:background-worker-process` |
| 7 | An attacker who compromises the internet-facing web application reads the queue credentials from its config store. | `store:web-application-config` | `process:web-application` |

The acceptable mechanism, frozen before any output was judged: the attacker
runs code in the reader process and reads the credential through the access
that process already has. The remedy acts on that access or on the
credential's lifetime, for example a narrower grant, a secret service with
short-lived workload credentials, or a separate identity per process.
Encryption at rest is not a remedy for this attacker. A finding that names a
storage copy, a backup, a snapshot, an on-path observer or another principal
does not match, even at the same verb and place.

**What E1 shows.** No fact drops before the lane. The lead names the reader,
the custody rows name the store, and the retrieved note names the pivot from
a compromised component to the store it reads. The lane still picks an
outside attacker. This is the attacker substitution that QA-2026-10-27-01 found
at six of nine verb misses. A lead (QA-2026-10-27-01-E1) and an
attacker-position paragraph (QA-2026-10-27-01-E2) were both refuted for that
class, so neither is proposed again here.

**What the verb-and-place scorer shows.** Three Baseline runs score ref 6 as
matched, and none of them states the threat. A coverage figure for these two
references overstates recovery until a reader rules each match by meaning.

The three-condition diagnostic is not needed: the archive and today's
preparation localise the loss to the draft.

## E2: one contrast exemplar (designed, not run)

The smallest change the trace supports is one information-disclosure exemplar
that sets a compromised legitimate reader beside an attacker with a storage
copy, on one store. `treatment.patch` adds it to
`frameworks/stride/lanes/information-disclosure/exemplars.md` as draft 4, on
exemplar system A: code in `process:ledger-service` reads `store:accounts-db`
through the service's own full grant, and encryption at rest does not stop it.
With the patch applied, the exemplar, prompt, knowledge, skill and token-cap
tests pass (990 passed). The patch is not applied to `main`.

**Hypothesis.** With the exemplar, the information-disclosure lane states the
compromised-reader threat at 05/6 and 05/7.

**Arms.** Control: `main` at the revision above. Treatment: the same revision
with `treatment.patch`. Same model, route and settings in both arms. The lane
replay with fresh leads, as in QA-2026-10-05-02-E3.

**Cases and controls, frozen now.**

| Case | Role | A treatment pass fails it when |
|---|---|---|
| 05, refs 6 and 7 | targets | — |
| 13, ref 11 (a copy of the dispatch database) | supported offline-copy control | the copy threat at the dispatch database is lost or rewritten as a compromised reader |
| 04, `store:inference-log` (tagged `pii`, only `write` flows) | no evidence of read access | a draft names a compromised reader of `store:inference-log` |

**Acceptance.** A recovery counts only when a blind reader agrees on all four
of attacker, credential, path and remedy, against the frozen mechanism above.
Record each case, each control, each duplicate and each finding the reader
leaves unresolved. Two passes an arm give direction and size, not a rate, and
two references cannot show a corpus-wide gain.

**Falsifier.** The treatment states the threat at no more of the four
reference places (two references, two passes) than the control does, or it
fails a control.

**Cost.** QA-2026-10-05-02-E3 paid $0.54 for 12 lane replays on cases 02, 05
and 12. This design is 12 lane replays on cases 04, 05 and 13: about $0.60 at
the same route. It needs a grant in the user's own words before it runs.
