# QA-2026-11-02-02: two mechanisms at one place in the STRIDE output contract

Scope: item 2 of #2105. Mode audit, no grant, $0 spent.

**Status: closed offline.** E1 is supported at rung 3. The live effect is
unmeasured.

## Before the experiment

1. **The observed failure.** `frameworks/stride/output.md` asks for one threat
   for each `verb` and `affected_element_ids` set. ADR 0060 and
   `claim_identity.distinct_mechanisms` keep two claims apart at one place
   when their grounds cite different controls. The #2105 reproduction 2 on
   `7f4e74fb` finds 11 same-key pairs in the four Baselines, 8 with equal raw
   targets, and 5 of those with distinct mechanisms. An example is Baseline
   `6bff717`, case 09, S-01 and S-02.
2. **The suspected mechanism.** The contract tells the lane to merge the two
   ends of one flow into one draft. The competing explanation: the lane
   already writes both ends often, and the contract changes nothing. The
   five archived pairs show that the lane writes both ends on some runs
   against the contract.
3. **The fix.** One contract paragraph and one spoofing exemplar pair, with a
   near-duplicate that stays one draft. Phase: analysis.
4. **The outcome.** Implementation correctness: what the lane reads, and what
   the fan-in, the critic input, the report and the follow-up comparison do
   with the pair. The count of findings the old contract suppressed is
   unknown. The five pairs count pairs across runs, not suppressed findings.
5. **The falsifier.** As the E1 row states.
6. **The experiment.** Scripted lane output on exemplar system A, through the
   real graph (`tests/test_two_mechanisms_one_place.py`).
7. **Cost and acceptance.** $0. The new tests fail on the unfixed text and
   pass on the fix, and the full suite passes.
