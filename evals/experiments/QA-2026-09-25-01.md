## What #926 already delivers of #741

#741 asks for an assertion catalog beside the graph: subject, stated fact,
exact location, explicit or inferred, scope, completeness limit and
contradictions. #926 ships each of these. An `Assertion` carries a subject, a
predicate, a value, a scope, a basis, a support span and an unknown reason.
`conflicts()` derives contradictions from the rows. A lane can cite a settled
row as `assertion:<id>`, and the critic rules against what the row says.

Three parts stay open:

- **The layer is off by default.** A lane reads the catalog only with
  `ANALYSIS_ASSERTIONS` set.
- **The critic reads no source text.** It sees a catalog row only where a
  draft cites one. A lane cannot inspect a conflict, because `settled`
  withholds both sides.
- **Some facts have no predicate.** Registry version 7 holds 21 predicates.
