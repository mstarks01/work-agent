# QA-2026-10-31-05: can a diagram's shapes state a type without harming a loose drawing?

Scope: two narrower follow-ups to QA-2026-10-31-04-E1, which fixed the clean
diagrams and harmed the drawn ones. Mode implement, one grant of $1.30,
$1.1742 metered at list price (an upper bound).

**Status: closed.** E1 is refuted by the keep rule, and E2 is inconclusive.
Neither candidate is kept, and the register stays as it is.

## Before the experiments

1. **The observed failure.** The register sentence of QA-2026-10-31-04 took
   every shape as a type. It cut elements lost on the clean diagrams from 7.75
   to 0.75 a sweep, and raised them on the drawn ones from 77.0 to 135.0,
   because a person uses plain and rounded boxes loosely.
2. **Two candidates, each one change on its own branch from `47e995b4`:**
   - **E1, cylinder only** (`candidate/diagram-cylinder-is-store`, `07134ed8`):
     "A node drawn as `id[(text)]` is a data store. No other shape states its
     type or an attribute." A cylinder means a store even in a loose drawing.
   - **E2, a stated convention** (`candidate/diagram-shape-convention-note`,
     `2c703c76`): the export's first line under `flowchart LR` is the comment
     `%% Shapes: id[name] is an external entity, id(name) a process, id[(name)]
     a data store.`, and the register says "Where a comment in the diagram
     states its shape convention, a node's shape states its type as the
     comment says. Otherwise the shape of a node states no attribute." The
     clean diagrams carry the comment; the drawn ones do not.
3. **The outcome.** Elements lost per sweep, as QA-2026-10-31-03 defines it.
4. **The falsifier, for each candidate.** On the clean set its mean is not
   lower than the before mean by half the before sd, or on the drawn set its
   mean exceeds the before mean by more than half the before sd.
5. **The before sides, archived.** Clean: QA-2026-10-31-03 `diagram-r1` to
   `r4` at `ab7dc98d`, mean 7.75, sd 2.75. Drawn: QA-2026-10-31-04
   `drawn-before-r1` to `r3` at `47e995b4`, mean 77.0, sd 3.46. No prompt byte
   differs between `ab7dc98d` and `47e995b4`.
6. **The after sides.** For each candidate, four sweeps over the clean set
   (for E2, the clean diagrams as its branch writes them, with the comment)
   and three over the drawn set `QA-2026-10-31-04/drawn`. All 16 cases,
   `gpt-5.6-luna` through OpenRouter, three cases in flight, the candidates in
   turn. A one-case pre-flight of E2's clean set comes first, because its text
   is new. Each arm is replayed with `run.py replay` over `evals/corpus`.
7. **Cost.** Metered from each artifact's tokens at OpenRouter's list price,
   an upper bound. The last sweeps read $0.083 to $0.091 each, so fourteen
   sweeps and a pre-flight read about $1.22. A sweep that would take the spend
   past $1.25 does not start; an arm short of its sweeps is inconclusive.

- **E1, refuted.** The cylinder sentence helps the clean diagrams a little

  (mistyped 1.33 to 4.0).
- **E2, inconclusive.** Its clean arm is the best figure of any arm so far,
  and the pre-flight typed case 10's CDN correctly for the first time. But the
  fourth clean sweep did not start: the metered spend was $1.174, and one more
  sweep would pass the declared $1.25 stop. The sweeps cost about $0.09 each,
  more than the estimate.
- **The drawn comparison is weaker than its line.** E2 cannot change how a
  drawn diagram reads, because the drawn diagrams carry no comment. Yet its
  drawn figure moved from 77.0 to 82.0. The drawn before side is archived from
  QA-2026-10-31-04, run an hour earlier and not in turn with these sweeps, and
  three sweeps give a band of 1.73. So part of each drawn "harm" reading may be
  drift. The declared rule still decides, so neither candidate is kept.
- **What the next run needs.** A drawn before arm run in turn with the
  candidate, with five or more sweeps, and the fourth E2 clean sweep.
