# QA-2026-11-13-01: a question kind ranks by the rules it informs

Scope: phase 1 of #2054 (ADR 0117), an offline comparison of four orders of
the early question list. The mode is offline, with no grant and no spend.

**Status: closed.** E1 is the default since #2249. The maintainer chose it
with its losses stated below.

## The orders

At ac9c65fe a question kind on the early list ranked by its prior alone. In
#2249 each rule names the predicates that inform it (`INFORMED_BY`), and a kind
ranks by the candidates whose rule names one of its parts.

`run.py early-policies` read each order over 194 archived reports: 163 STRIDE
and 31 ASVS, from the cases that have reports of both frameworks. Every
question has the answer "I don't know", and the budgets are 5 to 60 choices.
`reports.txt` lists the reports. The Report refuses 7 other reports in
`evals/runs`, so a run over that folder stops.

| Row | Order | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| E1 | a kind ranks by the rules that name one of its parts | refuted, shipped: see below |
| E2 | as E1, with only STRIDE rules | refuted: every figure equals E1's |
| E3 | as E1, with a kind's rows as one turn | refuted: see below |
| E4 | as E3, with the next unit's cost in the turn | refuted: STRIDE with ASVS completes 229 against 929 findings at 5 choices |

E1 against ac9c65fe, matched budgets:

| Selection | Gains | Losses |
| --- | --- | --- |
| STRIDE alone, findings completed | +14 at 15, +35 at 20, +6 at 30, +3 at 45 | −1 at 10, −1 at 60 |
| STRIDE with ASVS, findings completed | +18 at 20, +33 at 30, +13 at 45, +4 at 60 | −1 at 15 |
| ASVS with STRIDE, units settled | none | −3 at 15, −144 at 20, −15 at 30, −81 at 45, −46 at 60 |
| ASVS alone | none | none |

E3 settles more ASVS units than E1 at 15 to 60 choices. It settles fewer
than ac9c65fe at 20, 45 and 60 choices. It completes fewer STRIDE findings
than E1 at 30 and 45 choices.

The ASVS rows cannot cause the loss in E1. The ASVS prior holds no question
kind, so no ASVS rule meets a kind's field, and the ASVS `INFORMED_BY` rows do
not move the early order. E2 equals E1 by that construction, not by a
measurement. So the STRIDE rows cause the loss. No row isolates how they take
the ASVS choices.
